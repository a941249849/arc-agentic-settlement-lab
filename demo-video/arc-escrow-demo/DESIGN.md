## Style Prompt

ArcEscrow demo video. Dark product UI, sharp commercial workflow framing, restrained motion, no decorative filler. The video should feel like a builder walking a reviewer through a working stablecoin settlement product.

## Colors

- Canvas: #030712
- Panel: #07111f
- Text: #f8fafc
- Muted text: #94a3b8
- Arc cyan: #0ea5e9
- Approval green: #10b981
- Warning amber: #f59e0b

## Typography

- Sans: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif
- Monospace for hashes and evidence: Courier New, monospace

## What NOT to Do

- Do not present unrecorded tx hashes as completed.
- Do not over-explain the submission context to end users.
- Do not use decorative abstract animation instead of product flow.
- Do not make the video longer than needed.
